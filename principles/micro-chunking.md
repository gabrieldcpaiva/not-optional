# Micro-Chunking & Negative Space

## Rules

- No continuous body-text block longer than 4 lines.
- Longer content must be broken into short paragraphs or bulleted lists.
- Printable canvases should maintain at least 40% empty / negative space.

## Intent

Reduce cognitive load and executive overload for neurodivergent and high-stress users. Information should arrive in bite-sized, scannable units that can be processed even when the reader is tired or interrupted.

This is not aesthetic minimalism. It is functional accessibility.
