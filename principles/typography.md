# High-Legibility Typography

## Rules

- Body text minimum: 12 pt (print) / comfortable equivalent on screen.
- Large Print variants: 18 pt minimum.
- Line height: 1.5 for body text.
- Prefer high-legibility sans-serifs for body copy.
- Avoid tight tracking, decorative display fonts for long reading, and justified text (creates rivers).
- Headings may use a more distinctive serif or display face, but must remain clearly hierarchical.

## Intent

Reduce letter blurring, text freezing, and cognitive friction for dyslexic and fatigued readers. Typography is not decoration; it is an accessibility surface.
