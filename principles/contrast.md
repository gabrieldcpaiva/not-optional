# Off-Black / Off-White Contrast

## Rule

Avoid pure black (`#000000`) on pure white (`#FFFFFF`).  
Prefer a warm off-black on a warm off-white (example: Ink `#2C2A28` on Paper `#F7F3EE`).

This maintains very high contrast (often >13:1) while eliminating the visual vibration and eye strain that pure black/white pairs create for many readers, especially under fatigue.

## Intent

High contrast without harshness. The interface or printable should feel calm and tactile rather than clinical or aggressive.
