# Screen-Reader Audio Preview Synthesizer

Give low-vision or blind users an audio bridge to every printable. Before a sheet is printed or handed over, the user must be able to hear a clear, concise description of its physical layout, sections, and intended use.

## Core Enforcement Rules

- Every printable product should include a scannable QR code that links to a short audio description of that specific sheet.
- The audio description must explain spatial layout (top / middle / bottom, left-to-right flow), major visual elements, and the intended sequence of use in plain language.
- Complex diagrams must receive spoken alt-text that conveys structure and meaning without relying on visual inspection.
- Narration tone remains calm, low-demand, and free of medical jargon or exclamation points.
- QR codes must be placed in a consistent, high-contrast location that survives home printing and does not interfere with primary content or safe margins.

## Process

1. Ingest the printable design specification or layout description.
2. Identify the sheet’s spatial structure and key visual elements.
3. Draft a short narration script (approximately 30 seconds / 70–90 words).
4. Produce spoken alt-text for any complex diagram.
5. Specify QR placement and link target.
